Make a Complaint

The LCDA is committed to protecting the community from the harmful effects of pollution, bad roads, and drainage issues. We provide a simple system for members of the public to make complaints.

Who should I contact?

If you come across a community problem, the quickest way to fix it is to contact the LCDA directly. They may not be aware of the problem or its effects and will be happy to fix it.

What happens next?

When you report an issue to the LCDA, they will investigate the matter and take the appropriate action. They will keep you informed of progress when they have concluded their investigation.
